Rise Against

Rise Against is a band that has been around since 2004 bringing hits like Swing life away, give it all and a fan favorite at every show is Savior. The band is known for being politicially outspoken and never backing down from letting their voice be hear about the injustice in the world. Bringing the support to all who feel unheard and unseen. They have 10 albums and have no plans of stopping.
