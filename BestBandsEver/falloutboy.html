Fall Out Boy

Fall Out Boy is a rock band known for combining pop-punk, alternative rock, and catchy melodies.

The band became popular in the 2000s with songs that feature memorable choruses and creative lyrics.For me they are a soundtrack of my teen years.Sugar,We're going down is their first mainstream hit and even now is one of the songs played constantly at emonights across the states. If you have never heard them before that should be one of the first songs you hear.I Have fond memories of seeing them live at the first warped tour I went to.They now have released 8 albums and their latest release is SO Much (for) Starust in 2023 and currently are hinting at a new album or tour.
